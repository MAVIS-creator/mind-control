📋 Game Rules▼

🎮 Single Player

  • Match all pairs as fast as you can
  • Your best time & moves are saved

👥 Multiplayer

  • Take turns flipping 2 cards (30s per turn, 15s on Hard)
  • Match a pair → keep your turn
  • Most pairs wins!

🏆 Points System

  • 1st place: +3 pts
  • 2nd place: +2 pts
  • 3rd place: +1 pt

🔌 Disconnect & Reconnect

  • If you disconnect, the game pauses for all players
  • You have 30 seconds to reconnect
  • Reconnecting resumes the game where you left off

⚠️ Forfeit Rule

  • Leaving or failing to reconnect within 30s = forfeit
  • Forfeiting counts as a loss (0 pts)
  • If 2+ players remain, the game continues without you
  • If only 1 player remains, they win (+3 pts)
